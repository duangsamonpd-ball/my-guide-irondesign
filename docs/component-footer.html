Product Footer

A site-wide footer promotion. Two variants — Suite showcases the full Iron Suite brand; Default frames a specific product as part of the suite. Each product link is the Product hover sub-component.

Anatomy
Three structural zones.
1
Variant content area — the left headline; changes between Suite (brand logo) and Default (“[Product] is a part of IRONSUITE”).
2
CTAs — real Button instances, matching Figma, which composes the DS button here too: <Button variant="tertiary" size="lg"> “Get Full 10 products Suite” + <Button variant="ghost" size="lg"> “Start Free Trial”. Until 2026-08-03 these were hand-rolled .btn-dark / .btn-text-light rules that had drifted from the DS: gap 8 against 12, and the ghost one rendered as a 19px text link — no pill, no padding, and white where Figma paints on-action-tertiary blue.
!
Ghost hover is 3.83:1 on this band. At rest the blue label is 5.48:1 and clears AA. On hover the DS ghost turns the label --color-on-tertiary-hover (Iron Pink 500), which over the violet lands at 3.83:1 — under AA for 16px text, over the 3:1 large-text bar. This is the DS button behaving normally, not a footer-specific choice; it is the same brand pink already kept at 3.78:1 elsewhere on this page. Settled 2026-08-03: keep it — the DS button’s own behaviour, and the colour is the brand’s. Don’t re-raise it.
3
Product links — the 10-item list, each an instance of the Product hover sub-component.
Responsive
This band carries a single breakpoint: below --breakpoint-lg 1024 .ft-inner stacks to a column on a 32px gap and the divider between the halves is hidden. It was 640 until 2026-08-05, and that was wrong by 312px of viewport. The two halves need 953 together — .ft-left floors at 381 and .ft-right at 572 — so everything from 641 to 952, 768 included, rendered a row that did not fit. Because .footer-top is overflow: hidden that clipped instead of scrolling: product descriptions cut mid-word, no scrollbar, and document.scrollWidth clean the whole time, which is how nine gates and every render pass missed it. The preview below is what found it. One caveat worth carrying: at 1024 the halves fit 1022-into-1024, so there are 2px of slack and a longer product name would reopen this.
The product rows now wrap too. They were white-space: nowrap, which floored each row at its widest sentence (500px) and made it wider than its own column below 499 — clipped by 125px at 375 and 180px at 320, stacked or not. .fp-name is its own flex row, so IRONPDF never breaks apart; only the sentence after the separator reflows.
The preview is an <iframe> loading this same page at ?frame=, because an iframe is the only box on a page with a viewport of its own — the demo frames above are pinned to 1440 by .frame-scroll and will never show this. Nothing is duplicated to make it work.
768px
Drag across 1024 to see the row become a column, and down past 499 to watch the descriptions reflow rather than get cut. The handle takes arrow keys (±16, or ±1 with Shift), which is the only reliable way to land on an exact threshold.

When to use
◆ Suite
Use on the homepage and product-agnostic landing pages. Highlights the full 10-product Iron Suite brand — ideal when no single product is the focus.
● Default
Use on individual product pages (IronPDF, IronWord…). The headline names the current product and cross-sells the broader suite.
