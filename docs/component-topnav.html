Top Nav

The 40px corporate utility strip that sits above the product header: Iron Software lockup and company-level menu on the left, office address, Contact Us and the language picker on the right. Pair it above Product Menu for the full two-bar header.

Guidelines
✓ Do
  • Keep this strip for company-level destinations — product navigation belongs in Product Menu below it.
  • Check contrast behind transparent; the labels are white with no scrim of their own.
  • Drop the address before the menu when space runs out — it is the longest and least navigational item.
✗ Don't
  • Don’t recolour the Iron Software mark to white — it is --color-neutral here by design, recessive next to the wordmark.
  • Don’t use this strip on its own as a page’s only navigation.
  • Don’t stack more corporate links in; five is what the design budgets for at 1440px.
Notes
Figma specifies 1440px desktop only. The narrow-viewport behaviour here — the address goes at 1100px, the corporate menu at 720px — is an implementation decision, not a handed-over design.

Figma sets gap: 0 between a label and its caret and lets the caret’s own 16×16 box provide the spacing. The caret SVG therefore uses an offset viewBox to centre its 6.2×3.8 triangle inside 16×16, rather than adding a wrapper element.
