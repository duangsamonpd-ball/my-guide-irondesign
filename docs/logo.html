✳️ Logo

The Iron Software mark is the foundation of every product identity across the suite. This page documents the primary mark, its size & grid, the wordmark lockups, all 24 product marks in colour and mono, product-specific lockups, the illustrative product elements, and common misuse to avoid. Every asset here is exported from Figma — no CSS filters standing in for a real variant.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Clear space = height of one mark segment (X). Keep this zone free of text, edges, and other logos.

Minimum size
16px — too small
24px — minimum
48px — comfortable
Size & Grid
24PXGRID 1PX
48PXGRID 2PX
96PXGRID 4PX
192PXGRID 8PX

The mark is drawn on an 8-unit grid at 192px. Each halving of the render size halves the grid unit, so edges stay on whole pixels at 96, 48 and 24px — the four sizes above are the only ones that land cleanly.

Lockup Patterns
Basic — horizontal

Mark left, name and edition stacked to its right. Use in headers, nav bars, and anywhere vertical space is tight.

Stack — vertical

Mark above, name and edition centred below. Use on cards, product tiles, and square-ish spaces.

Product Elements

Illustrative marks used alongside — never instead of — the product logo: feature cards, empty states, docs headers. A separate family from the square product marks above.

Don'ts
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't rearrange elements
Keep the mark and wordmark in fixed relative position — never split or reorder them.
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't recolor the mark
Only use approved brand color tokens — never arbitrary or off-brand hues.
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't add effects
No drop shadows, glows, gradients, or bevels on the mark or wordmark.
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't use busy backgrounds
Avoid photos, patterns, or gradients that fight the mark for attention.
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't use low contrast
Always check contrast against the background — the mark must stay legible.
✕
IRONSOFTWARE
Don't distort proportions
Never stretch or squash the mark — scale it uniformly only.
